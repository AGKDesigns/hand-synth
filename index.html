Hand Synth

Your right hand owns the chord. Hold up one finger for the I, two for the II, and so on up to seven. Tilt that hand either way to get the other quality — major becomes minor, minor becomes major — and raise or lower it to swell the volume.

Your left hand only modifies. Its four fingers count the voicing — one for the plain triad, two for a first inversion, three for a seventh, four for a dominant or diminished seventh — its thumb jumps an octave, its tilt opens and closes the filter, and its height feeds the echo. Out of frame you get plain triads and nothing else changes.

Space pauses. Swap hands if the roles come out backwards.
